1. CGPA Distribution — Histogram
Frequency distribution of cumulative CGPA scores across the filtered cohort
What it shows The frequency distribution of student cumulative CGPAs across the filtered cohort, divided into grade intervals. When a student is selected, their CGPA bin is explicitly highlighted.
Why it's useful Helps students understand the overall shape of academic attainment (median, spread, skewness) and determine whether grades cluster in upper or lower bands.
What to look for Where your score sits relative to the peak (mode) and the cohort mean (dashed vertical line), and whether the majority of peers score above or below your bin.

Performance Categories & Definitions:
What it shows The proportion and count of students classified into each performance tier: Distinction, Upper Credit, Lower Credit, Merit, Pass, and Below Pass.
Why it's useful Summarizes overall performance category distribution and shows the proportion of students attaining upper-tier academic standing.
What to look for The proportion of students achieving credit or distinction standing (CGPA ≥ 3.50). Click any slice or legend item to filter the dashboard.
2. Program-wise Average CGPA — Bar Chart
Filtered cohort — sorted descending by mean CGPA

What to look for Where your programme ranks relative to others, and the cohort sample size (n) behind each bar. Click any bar to filter the dashboard by that programme.
5. Graduation Year vs Average CGPA — Line Chart
Chronological progression of mean CGPA across graduating cohorts (2010–2014)

Why it's useful Demonstrates batch-to-batch stability and cohort attainment trends across the 5-year academic study period.
What to look for This chart shows differences between graduation cohorts. It describes variation in average performance but does not establish why the differences occurred. Click any point to filter by graduation year.

4. SGPA vs CGPA — Scatter Plot
Individual student coordinates comparing cumulative CGPA with final semester SGPA
What it shows Individual student positions comparing cumulative CGPA (x-axis) with final semester SGPA (y-axis), alongside a 1:1 diagonal reference line.
Why it's useful Identifies final-semester performance dynamics—dots above the diagonal indicate students whose final semester improved over their cumulative baseline, while dots below reflect a final-semester decline.
What to look for Your highlighted amber position. Note: this visualizes descriptive correlation, not academic causation. Click any dot to view that student's complete profile.
